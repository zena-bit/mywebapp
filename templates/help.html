Payments

We accept M-Pesa STK Push (the most popular), as well as bank transfers. Simply enter your M-Pesa number at checkout and approve the prompt on your phone.

All payments are processed securely via the Safaricom M-Pesa API. We never store your M-Pesa PIN or any card details. Your transactions are fully encrypted end-to-end.

If payment fails, ensure you have sufficient M-Pesa balance and your number is registered. You can retry from your orders page. If it persists, contact our support team via WhatsApp.

Delivery

Nairobi orders are delivered within 1–2 business days. Countrywide deliveries take 3–5 business days depending on location.

Returns & Refunds

Contact us via WhatsApp or email within 30 days of delivery. Items must be in original condition with packaging. We'll arrange pickup at no extra cost.

Once we receive the returned item, refunds are processed within 3–5 business days directly to your M-Pesa number.
